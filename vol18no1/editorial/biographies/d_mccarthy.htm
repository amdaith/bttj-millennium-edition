Millennium Edition Project Team

Des McCarthy BSc DMS, Project Manager

After joining BT as an apprentice in 1980, and later graduating, Des worked at Adastral Park on the implementation of the Telephony over a Passive Optical Network (TPON) Project.

Since 1991 Des has been an internal management consultant working on Total Quality Management, Business Excellence, and Programme and Project Management. Assignments during this last year have included: establishing the Applied Research and Technology (ART) Millennium Dome Project, development of Infrastructure and Communications within Advanced Communications Research (ACR), management of a multidisciplinary international team to successfully deliver the global Concert Integrated Messaging System used throughout 17 BT Global Communities, delivery of the world's first multimedia technology journal - the BT Technology Journal Millennium Edition, and the commercialisation of research capabilities.
