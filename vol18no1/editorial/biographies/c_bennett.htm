Chris Bennett, Resourcing

Chris is a third year undergraduate at the University of Central England, studying towards a BA (Hons) in Media and Information. During an eight-month industrial placement at BT, he has been employed as a trainee Information Officer working in the Information Office at Adastral Park, which provides a technical and commercial information service to BT personnel. Chris was responsible for locating and obtaining copies of seminal papers, which form part of the Yesterday section, along with the relevant copyright details. In addition to information and BTTJ Y2K duties he is undertaking an evaluation project of the BT Digital Library through server log file and site traffic analysis. Before going to university, Chris also spent five years in the Royal Navy as a Communications Mechanic.
