Richard Nicol PhD CEng FIEE

As BT's Head of Research, Richard Nicol now manages the central research team in Advanced Communications Engineering (ACE) and is the functional leader of the 600 people working on BT's research programme across N&IS. He joined the Post Office Research Department's video telecommunications division in 1970 to study the then new area of video data compression, including the Interframe video compression systems, for which he gained a PhD in 1975. He was subsequently involved in early multimedia research and in developing international standards for low-bit rate video transmission. He also led the development of market-leading videoconference codec and multipoint conferencing products. After three years managing BT's university research programme, he returned in 1992 to head a £20 million work programme including a high-profile multimedia ISDN communications system for the PC, video on demand and integrated home broadband services. In the same year, he was awarded the Martlesham Medal for his work on digital video conferencing systems.

He later became the head of operations of the Applied Research and Technology Department (ART) - the 500-strong research and advanced development organisation at Adastral Park. He is a visiting Professor at the University of Essex and an honorary Professor at the University of Lancaster.

You can contact Richard at richard.nicol@bt.com.
