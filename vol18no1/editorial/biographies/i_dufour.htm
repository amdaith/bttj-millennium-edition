Editorial Board

I G Dufour EurIng CEng FIEE

Ian Dufour is head of the Central Design Group, and chief designer, in Advanced Communications Engineering (ACE).

Since joining BT as an apprentice, Ian has held a range of engineering posts, working on digital transmission systems, switching systems, external plant, computerised planning systems and local loop planning. He played a leading role in the widespread provision of digital transmission systems across the UK including the early implementation of single-mode optical fibres, the deployment of local-loop electronics and the introduction of BT's ISDN service.

In 1991, Ian set up the network integration facility at Martlesham and prior to his current appointment managed a number of organisations working in the area of intelligent networks, mobile networks, signalling and the Internet.

Ian is also responsible for running the BT MSc, an in-house modular MSc in telecommunications, run in conjunction with the University of London. He sits on the editorial board of the BT Technology Journal and is editor of a book on network intelligence. He is a European Engineer, a Chartered Engineer and a Fellow of the IEE.
