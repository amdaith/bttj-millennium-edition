The Past

Gerry White, Chairman of the BT Technology Journal

 

When we decided to put together this special millennium edition we envisioned an edition primarily concerned with telecommunications as it is today and tomorrow.

To understand where we are today, however, and where we may be tomorrow, we need to understand where we have come from. In the field of telecommunications, the dominant thread of its history is related to the invention of the telephone and the history of telephony. The coming of the so- called 'data wave' will change all of this and perhaps, in a history viewed from 50 years in the future, voice communications will be relegated to a similar marginal position that we now give to Morse code telegraphy! Nevertheless, the underlying developments in technologies, used to transduce, transmit, route and manage, remain essentially common to the general history of all telecommunications.

Telephony is now over 120 years old and is the product of a whole industry from around the world. In this history, however, there have been seminal contributions by key individuals now regarded as the giants of the science of communications. Each student of history will view the past with a different perspective and undoubtedly identify a different set of names for the key contributors.

Despite these differing perspectives, an attempt has been made to identify a small subset of historic contributors that most would agree have played pivotal roles in the creation and development of the telecommunications network as we now see it. If we have omitted some that other observers would have included, or included some that others would not, we can only apologise for the inevitably personal nature of our selection.

The developments of telecommunications stand, of course, on the foundations of the advances in understanding of the science of electricity and magnetism and it would therefore be inappropriate not to include, in this listing, the seminal developments in these areas of the physical sciences.

This distillation of the history of telecommunications past therefore starts with this general background in the electrical sciences before proceeding to a selection of the key steps in the development of the science and technology of telecommunications which have led to man's most complex creation, the world telecommunications network.

Following these key papers, there is a listing of the landmark BT developments right up to 1999.
