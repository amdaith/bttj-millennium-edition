Gerry White

Gerry White is a Company Consultant to BT at Adastral Park where he is Chairman of the BTTJ Editorial Board. He has over thirty years experience in the Telecommunications Industry in both the United Kingdom and the United States. His experience has been both in manufacturing industry and in operating companies with major specialisation in research, development and product design.

He led teams responsible for the early introduction of digital switching in both public (System X) and private (Monarch) switch products. He was co-recipient of the Design Council Award for his leadership of the design of the all digital Monarch PBX. He has held senior positions in the Industry and was until recently General Manager of the Research Department at BT's Laboratories at Martlesham where he also had special responsibility for BT's development activities in North America.

Prior to his career with BT he was employed with the AT&T Bell Telephone Laboratories, New Jersey. He holds degrees in Electronic Engineering and Mathematics at Bachelor's, Doctor's and Post-Doctoral levels. He has been widely involved in professional activities internationally and is associated, in various senior membership capacities, with the leading electrical and telecommunications professional societies.

Dr White has received a number of honours and awards and is currently an Honorary Fellow of the University of Wales, Bangor where he is also an Honorary Professor.
