'An Investigation of the Laws of Thought'

G Boole

Synopsis

The twentieth century has seen many major changes in the nature of work and its role in people's lives. Organisational change has been driven by dramatic changes in the economic and social climate during this period. This is happening at an increasingly rapid rate as we approach the end of the century. This paper reviews current trends in the world of work. It will focus on the increasing dispersal of the workforce and how communications technology makes possible working arrangements which were previously unthinkable.
