Introduction

Stewart Davies, Director of Advanced Communications Engineering
 

BT's vision is to be '...the most successful world-wide communications group'. This means growing in scale, increasing our global presence, building our brand and reputation, being number one in customer satisfaction and creating greater shareholder value.

In order to achieve this we need to identify the best opportunities for growth in the market-place and invest quickly and aggressively. Fundamental to this is our ability to capitalise on the proliferation of the Internet and become 'New Wave'.

A New Wave communications company is one which invests for the future, drives for shareholder value, is quick to move into new markets and understands the value in these markets. Its people are market focused, entrepreneurial and quick to react.

A New Wave communications company cannot survive without having leading edge technology, and engineering capability, at its foundation and without clearly understanding the impact of future technology. This is the strategic vision that Advanced Communications Engineering provides to BT. ACE leads for BT on innovation, and driving change through technology.

The telecommunications industry has never before experienced the type and rate of change that it is seeing today. With the emergence of new technology and new markets we are seeing disintermediation (businesses having to restructure due to Internet technology), convergence (new applications enabling voice, moving images and text in real time) and fragmentation (where traditional business are now able to offer products and services in completely new market places).

This presents us with challenges and opportunities at all levels in the business and is driven by incredible rates of technological change and innovation. We've broken down the challenges into a number of key strategic thrusts each recognising the key technology areas enabling BT to be a New Wave communications company:

  • data,
  • mobility,
  • Internet and multimedia applications (including eCommerce),
  • delivery of customer solutions.

To deliver new products and services based on this technology needs global alliances and partnerships, collaboration and a new level of innovation and creativity in our people. ACE provides a key differentiator for BT in all these areas and is making us truly New Wave.
