David Smith

As general manager for Internet & Data Networks in Advanced Communications Engineering (ACE), David Smith is responsible for IP and data network and system design, and the supporting access and core transmission infrastructures for the BT Group.

Joining the PO Research Department (later BT Research Labs) in the early 1970s to work on optical communication systems, he was part of the pioneering optical-fibre trials and took a leading role in developing optical receivers.

David later led the BT team which formed BT&D Technologies, a joint venture company set up with Du Pont to develop and manufacture opto-electronic components.

Two years after forming the Datacommunications Division of BT Labs in 1991 he was appointed general manager for the Network Engineering Department responsible for the Network Transport, Network Intelligence and Network Applications Engineering Centres. In 1996, David was appointed general manager for Broadband & Data Solutions in networkBT responsible for the design, delivery and in-life management of BT UK's private circuit products, advanced data services and IP services.

You can contact David at david.r.smith@bt.com.
