Data Transport

David Smith, Manager of Internet & Data Networks
 
IP services, driven by the explosive growth in public Internet and business intranet applications, is the fastest growing area of the telecommunications business with traffic growth exceeding 10% per month. This growth will continue to accelerate over the coming few years as eBusiness and eCommerce applications transform the way our customers work and buy goods and services. To meet this demand, BT is deploying a 'state of the art' multi-gigabit IP network in the UK, and globally through Concert, our joint venture company with AT&T. In the UK, this new core network will be completed by Spring 2000, and will give more than 60-fold increase in capacity over our existing IP network. The core routers, each capable of routeing at more than 40 Gbit/s are interconnected by dense wavelength division multiplex (DWDM) transmission systems which will initially provide 5 Gbit/s of capacity on each fibre pair. Access into the network will be further enhanced by significantly increasing the modem ports at our exchanges to terminate dial-in traffic. In addition, higher speed Internet access will become available through the deployment of ADSL (asymmetric digital subscriber loop) technology over our existing copper access network giving a 20-fold increase in speed over the PSTN modems available today.
