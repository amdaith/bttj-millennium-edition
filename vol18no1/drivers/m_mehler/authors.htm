Mick Mehler

Mick Mehler heads up Advanced Communication Engineering's (ACE) Mobility and Network Services division which designs and delivers BT's mobility, intelligence and middleware platforms.

After graduating in 1980 Mick worked on microwave antenna design for both ground-based and satellite applications.

In 1984 he joined the University of Birmingham as a lecturer in their electronic and electrical engineering department. His research interests were in the fields of electromagnetics, antennas, propagation and high temperature superconductivity. He rejoined BT Labs in 1989 to head up the new Radio Science Group which was involved on early work in the development of software tools used by both the BT Group and Cellnet.

Before taking up his present position, Mick was for six years programme manager of BT's £25 million Network Corporate Research Programme. One of his responsibilities was to run the benefits analysis unit, linking the R&D programme closely with BT Group's corporate objectives. He was also responsible for implementing a third-generation R&D management methodology, collaborating closely with the European Industrial Research Management Association and the Arthur D Little consultancy group.

You can contact Mick at mick.mehler@bt.com.
