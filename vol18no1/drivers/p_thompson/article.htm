Solutions

Peter Thompson , Manager of Integration & Customer Solutions
 

Analysis has shown that technology has moved ahead at such a pace that organisations are finding it increasingly difficult to know how to specify their requirements in a market-place that offers so much functionality and so many options. To de-risk the purchase decision, large corporates are already asking for detailed consultancy in all aspects of the procurement of technology. More and more frequently the decision is at Chief Executive level rather than at IT Director level, given that technology embraces all aspects of their business, e.g. eBusiness and eCommerce.

The communications companies that will win in this solutions market-place will be those who are able to present seamless service propositions - dynamically, flexibly and cost effectively - while managing and protecting the customer from the underlying integration complexities.

Being at the heart of the R&D world, Advanced Communications and Engineering (ACE) people are fully aware of the technology that is emerging from the development pipeline. ACE people are therefore in a unique position to provide the technical innovation to customers who want the benefit of new, integrated communications technology and business applications.

There is no other major communications supplier that has its R&D arm integrated with the business as much as BT's - BT provides a more complete design, implement, deliver and support capability than almost any competing organisation.

But solutions are not just the domain of the large corporates; the demand for solutions embraces the whole business market - from, on the one hand, global corporations, to, on the other hand, small and medium enterprises who want the benefits of that technology without the risk of owning it.

ACE at Adastral Park is the benchmark for seamless integration of complex services and will be critical to the successful delivery of the solutions that will provide BT's customers with enduring, seamless services adding significant value to their business.
