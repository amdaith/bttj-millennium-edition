Internet, Multimedia and eCommerce

Bob Foster , Manager of Internet & Multimedia Applications
 
The Internet is about to take off.

That may be a strange thing to say about a phenomenon that has been growing so rapidly for the past few years but, as someone once said, "You ain't seen nothing yet."

There are at least four new factors which will drive the growth far beyond what we have seen so far.

  • The availability of new terminals.
    Until now the Internet has been the preserve of PC users but now new devices like TV set top boxes, web phones, PDAs and public kiosks are making the internet available to more people. People like my Mum who has never wanted a PC but now wants to send e-mail to her friends.
  • The introduction of higher speed access networks.
    BT's new Highway broadband service which uses ADSL provides access more than ten times faster than was possible even over the ISDN. Similarly cable modems will offer high speeds over cable TV networks and even mobile access will be much faster thanks to the launch of GPRS and UMTS services. This higher speed doesn't just mean pages download faster, but they also enable many new services to be provided, services that benefit from high quality audio or video, like home working.
  • New 'always on' services.
    Most of the new higher speed services will be subscription based rather than charge for usage. This makes it practical to leave the connection open permanently and use the network for things like security cameras or remotely controlling home appliances.
  • The growth of eCommerce.
    eCommerce is growing rapidly but credit cards are not an efficient way of paying small amounts of money. New micropayment services like BT's Worldpay allow small payments to be handled efficiently and will greatly increase the trading of information on the Internet.

The overall effect of this is that the Internet will increasingly impact on every aspect of our lives. This in turn represents a huge opportunity for BT, to expand from a business which today is primarily a telephone service, to providing a much richer range of communication, information, entertainment and trading services.

Our role in ACE is to keep BT ahead of the game in this fast-moving world, identifying the trends and acquiring the best technologies, as someone else once said, "It's a tough job but someone's got to do it."
