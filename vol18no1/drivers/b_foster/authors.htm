Bob Foster

As general manager of Advanced Communication Engineering's (ACE) Internet and Multimedia Applications, Bob Foster is responsible for the design, building and support of a wide range of products based on Internet and multimedia technologies for the BT group.

He has held a number of positions in BT with responsibility for product development. He has established a reputation for the rapid development and deployment of new products including, Talking Pages, Callminder, Caller ID, The Interactive TV trial, BT Net, BT Internet, Campus World, Webworld, Wireplay, ClickFree, Talk21 and Trustwise.

Bob is a member of the Global Internet Project, and many years ago led the work to develop the European videotex standard which was adopted by the PTTs in The Netherlands, Germany, Austria and Switzerland. He has been a consultant to the EU and a number of overseas telecommunications companies. He has a first-class degree in electrical engineering from Imperial College, London, is a Member of the IEE, a Chartered Engineer, Associate of the City and Guilds of London Institute and holds a Diploma in Accountancy and Finance. Bob acts as the BT senior manager for the Ipswich area and is a guardian of the Ipswich foyer and Vice President of the talking Newspaper for the Blind. He lives in Dedham in Essex in a Georgian house he restored from a derelict condition and his other hobbies are the theatre and sailing.

You can contact Bob at bob.foster@bt.com.
