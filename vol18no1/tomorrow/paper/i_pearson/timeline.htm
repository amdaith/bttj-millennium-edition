BT Technology Timeline - Towards Life in 2020

The Timeline

About half of the entries in the 1999 timeline came from BT's researchers at Adastral Park, many from my own studies. The other half have been gleaned from the press or conferences over the last few years, but they are just a small fraction of all the predictions I came across. Many weren't quoted in this timeline because I was unhappy about the credibility of the source. Others were too vague to be useful or didn't give any clues as to when they might occur. Others were just silly and belong more to sci-fi than real futurology.

This time I have quoted a single date for each item. There is always some uncertainty about development rates. Public acceptance, political considerations, marketing and many other factors also affect the likely date for achieving a particular event. The date I quote is just my best guess, and generally the further in the future it is, the less accurate it is likely to be. Previous versions have been pretty close so far, but it is too early to say how good the long term guesses have been. The consolation is that for many of the longer term items, it is more the fact that they could happen than when they will happen that is significant. Some items sound too good to be true, but we must remember one thing. It is easy to take account of the barriers and bottlenecks caused by physics and engineering, that slow down or block developments. But no amount of foresight can anticipate what will be invented in a few years by some 21st century genius. The items listed here (figure 1) are based on known science and technology. The future may look very much more exciting as our knowledge improves.
