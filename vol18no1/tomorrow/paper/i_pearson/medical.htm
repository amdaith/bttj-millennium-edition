BT Technology Timeline - Towards life in 2020

Medical Technology

Of course, medical technology is improving rapidly too, and by 2020, new babies can expect to live to well over 100, perhaps 130 might be common. In fact, for a while, life expectancy will increase faster than people get older. By the time today's babies are due to die we will probably be able to download their minds into electronic storage and they will be able to carry on digitally even when their bodies are dead and buried. Imagine making a speech at your own funeral! Many of today's children will survive long enough to enjoy this effective immortality.

With a complete map of the human genome and some important animals, we will be able to customise our children and pets. This technology is quite disturbing to many people and such developments are likely to be resisted in many countries. However, it is also likely that someone somewhere will offer such a service, at a price. More down to earth, our understanding of the body will be much greater then, and we will be able to treat many more conditions, much more successfully than today. More than 95% of body weight is made up of bits that could be replaced by synthetic alternatives by 2020! Many organs, and perhaps even limbs, could be replaced by fully organic replacements, grown in the lab.

Information technology will help enormously. Apart from having a full multimedia medical record, including your operation videos and scans, we will perhaps routinely wear health monitors. Our electronic environment will react to our emotional and physical state automatically, helping us reduce stress. By linking directly to our nervous system, computers could pick up what we feel, and hopefully be able to stimulate feeling too, so that we can start to develop full sensory virtual environments, rather like the holidays on Total Recall or the Star Trek holodeck. This will be the beginning of the long process of man-machine convergence that will culminate in a fully electronic human well before the end of the next century.
