Assessing the Risk

Even with all the embedded systems identified the problem did not end there. You may not have had time to test and check every Embedded System that you and your team found so at the end of the day you had to examine your priorities and what would happen in the event of a failure.

  • What would happen to your business if that piece of equipment failed?
  • Was it connected to any safety critical equipment HSE? [12]
  • Was it just a minor inconvenience should it fail? Could the date just be wound back - did this have any legal implications?
  • Would the unit have been replaced under your normal upgrade/replacement cycle - was it due, was it brought forward?
  • Did the original supplier of the equipment provide you with an acceptable compliance statement or data?

Assessing all of the above allowed you to construct some form of 'Risk Matrix' for your business sector to be prepared, perhaps as follows:
