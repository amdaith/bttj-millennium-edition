Knowledge Management: the Tools for the Business

Knowledge Garden

The Knowledge Garden is a research project that aims to provide a source of shared information whereby users can meet and communicate. It brings together a number of media, including text, audio and video. The garden metaphor was considered appropriate for a variety of reasons. The concept of a garden is highly cross-cultural, and it reflects the organic, changing nature of information. It is envisaged that you will be able to 'grow' information plants in the garden based on your personal interests. When these plants get too large, cuttings can be taken forming the basis of new plants in the garden. .

The objective is to develop unified 2-D/3-D shared, dynamic and intelligent WWW-based information interfaces and representations. The project focuses on the user exploration and sharing of Internet-based information resources through this interface.

The Knowledge Garden enables the user to:

  • access large amounts of information via a natural and intuitive 3D interface,
  • collaborate, interact and communicate with other users,
  • have shared access to live, self-updating information 'trees',
  • communicate (via audio, video, e-whiteboards, etc) with others who share the garden.

It is proposed to make part of the Knowledge Garden a 3-D representation of the pages stored in the Jasper system, providing a logical, intuitive and accessible store of information. Such an arrangement is achieved by using the meta-information in the Jasper store, and results from an automatic clustering process whereby Internet page addresses that point to related information are grouped together. The resulting cluster structures of the Jasper store are thus used to create a novel 3D front-end for the Jasper system.
