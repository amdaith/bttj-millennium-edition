Jasper

The Internet is undeniably a vast information resource, but the hundreds of millions of documents it contains may be inaccessible due to their sheer quantity. However, they can be accessed usefully by Jasper information agents, the capabilities of which can be summarised as:

  • knowing the user's interests and representing these in a user profile,
  • modifying a user's profile based on the pages they view on the Internet,
  • communicating automatically with other users to share information,
  • summarising information for local storage,
  • extracting key words for indexing and later retrieval of information.

KSE, Jasper's successor, takes the Jasper concept to the next level using intelligent software agents to support WWW-based virtual communities of practice.

ProSearch

How often have you searched the Internet only to end up with spurious results that match your query but not your actual interests? BT's proactive search agent, ProSearch, will search this vast source of information on your behalf and then present you with results based on a profile of your personal interests. Your profile will be maintained by the agent and automatically clustered into related topic areas before searching begins. The results from each search are then scored, based on your whole profile, and given an overall ranking. Complementing the Internet search, you can also configure a selection of news or internal intranet sites enabling you to extract stories of interest, again related to your profile.

To avoid the problem of duplicate links, ProSearch will select only new or changed pages when searching on your behalf by checking each link every time the process is run. In addition, ProSearch will eliminate frustrating 'dead links' from your search results. The main proactive searching process may be run overnight, if required, as no user interaction is needed. When the search is complete, you can view the results using a standard Web browser. The results are shown as links to the respective Internet pages, together with their associated ratings. To save you even more time, a summary of each page is generated by ProSum so that you can gain a quick impression of the page without having to wait for it to down-load.

ProSum

The huge amount of information available today means that many of us suffer from information overload. You only have to think of the daily bombardment of literature that faces you at work each day, all to be read in an effort to keep up to date in your particular field. Even at home, you probably feel besieged by newspapers, advertising, consumer guides and so on.

BT's researchers at Adastral Park have developed the automatic text summarisation tool ProSum to help overcome your problems. ProSum can summarise any Internet page to a length specified by you. It can also go a stage further, summarising a document taking into account your specified interests and preferences. In addition, ProSum can produce a digest of information from more than one document.
