Videoconferencing for Speech and Language Therapy in Schools

Evaluation

Following an invitation to tender, the University of Glasgow [17] was contracted to provide an independent evaluation of the TeachSpeech project. This evaluation is currently ongoing. The four evaluation criteria are:

  • quality of the therapy;
  • cost of the therapy;
  • clinical effectiveness of the therapy being delivered via the TeachSpeech systems;
  • time allocated to each child.

Knowledge and understanding of these criteria will assist with determining whether or not the TeachSpeech concept is both practicable and viable. The evaluation considers two models of therapy delivery, the novel TeachSpeech method and the traditional face-to-face method. Measurement of the traditional method is being made using a control group model, consisting of eighteen children who are receiving speech and language therapy services from the nearby Salisbury NHS Health Trust. The TeachSpeech group currently contains eleven children dispersed across the four remote schools. The evaluation is using a combination of research methods, including:

  • log sheets, to measure the time allocated and how it is used with each child. These are completed by the SLTs in both models. The log sheets will also be used to calculate the cost of the therapy;
  • clinical assessment measures using the Enderby Therapy Outcome Measures tool [15];
  • semi-structured interviews, to assess equipment usability, user confidence, and provide 'quality of therapy' data. Interviews are carried out with the SLTs, ESAs, parents and teachers;
  • questionnaires to ESAs, SLTs and parents: to assess user satisfaction, provide information regarding working environments and assist the quality of therapy assessment;
  • content analysis: analysis of both direct and time-lapsed recordings of therapy sessions;
  • professional days - questionnaires are given to professionals who visit the I CAN Salisbury nursery for a demonstration of the TeachSpeech project. Opinion is sought with regard to general views about videoconferencing technology and its perceived value in relation to the project both before and after demonstration.

For each child, data is gathered for six months before being used for analysis purposes. Currently, this results in eight TeachSpeech and fourteen control group children qualifying for data analysis purposes to date. These numbers are expected to increase as the project progresses.
