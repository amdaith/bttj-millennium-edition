Videoconferencing for Speech and Language Therapy in Schools

Authors

Duncan Rose
 

Duncan Rose joined BT Laboratories in 1988. He worked initially on data modulator and demodulator DSP coding techniques and developed an ultra-high-speed digital equaliser for use in satellite systems. He has also investigated the application of signal processing techniques for the benefit of hearing impaired telephony users. In 1994 he developed telepresence systems to increase the realism of videoconferencing systems by the provision of eye-to-eye contact. In 1996 he was awarded a MSc in Telecommunications Engineering. Since 1997 he has also been working extensively on the BT CAREnet project, a research project exploring the application of communications technology for supporting older people in the community.

 

Stephen Furner
 

As a consultant Engineering Psychologist at BT Adastral Park, Stephen Furner has applied advanced usability engineering techniques to the research, design and development of BT products and services. He has led multinational teams of technical experts researching convergent technologies, and has managed national and international R&D projects for BT.

Stephen regularly reports the results of the research he carries out for BT at professional conferences, in technical journals and through the technical press and public media. His current research interests include computer haptics, social uses of computing and communications, self-positioning mobile data services and human cognition and perception for future convergent technologies.

Stephen is both a Chartered Psychologist and an Incorporated Engineer. He originally joined BT as a trainee technician in London, working on the development of the UK Telex network.

 

Alex Hall
 

After qualifying as a speech and language therapist in 1984 Alex Hall worked as a clinician for various NHS Trusts, gradually specialising in the specific speech and language difficulties of children and young people. From 1989 Alex managed a speech and language service as well as carrying her own caseload, and undertaking a Master's Degree in management. In this role she became increasingly aware of the need to develop new approaches to help children with communication problems. In 1994, Alex joined I CAN. She is responsible for I CAN's growing national network of Early Years Services, and for developing and evaluating I CAN's speech and language services in mainstream schools.

 

Karen Montgomery
 

Karen Montgomery is a speech and language therapist working on the TeachSpeech project in Salisbury. She has been using the videoconference terminals to support children at four mainstream schools during the past three years. Karen trained as a speech and language therapist and teacher in the United States receiving a Masters degree in 1980 from Northwestern University in Evanston, Illinois. She has worked with children in England since 1985 as an NHS therapist, privately and for I CAN. Karen is a registered member of the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists and the American Speech, Language and Hearing Association.

 

Elisabeth Katsavrasj
 

Elisabeth Katsavras graduated from the University of Glasgow with a 2.1 honours BSc in Psychology. After graduating she joined the Multimedia Communication Group at the University of Glasgow as a part-time research assistant. She is also employed part-time as the evaluator of the Teach Speech project. Working in an interdisciplinary team, her interests lie in fine grain analysis of dialogues. This type of analysis looks at the impact of technology on users. The goal is to help design communication technologies which are effective and easy to use.

 

Phil Clarke
 

Phil Clarke joined BT Labs in 1988 as a software engineer, working on network application platforms. In 1992 he moved into future applications and systems research, maintaining an interest in bridging the divide between technologies and customers. Since 1998 he has been developing and implementing advanced internet standards and protocols. He has a BSc in Software Engineering plus Psychology (Open University) and a MSc in Telecommunications Engineering (University of London), and started the world's first football website.
