Streaming Multimedia for the Internet

Intranet TV

Intranet TV is an intranet-based application that demonstrates the use of video-streaming technologies within the corporate environment [4]. Information providers can use Intranet TV to deliver audio and video based communications direct to the workplace desktop via the corporate LAN or to home-based workers via remote access ISDN or PSTN links. Typical corporate applications of Intranet TV include:

  • business television, providing access to the latest corporate news throughout the organisation,
  • training, enabling employees to learn new skills or refresh/reinforce existing skills online,
  • briefings, creating a channel for senior managers to communicate to distributed teams,
  • off Air TV, delivering TV to the desk for employees who need to watch specialist TV stations.

BT's Business TV programme vision has been available on-line through the Intranet TV site since October 1996. Figure 5 shows the vision programme running at 450 kbit/s on the Intranet TV site.

The Intranet TV site is regularly used to deliver live and on-demand access to department briefings at Adastral Park enabling people to time shift and space shift the way they receive this type of information. Selected off-air TV channels are also available. A recent enhancement to the Intranet TV site is a capability to webcast live events from external conference venues direct to the desktop.

Figure 6 shows a screen shot of the BT Alliance Engineering Symposium held in Oslo at the beginning of June 1999. During the three-day event, 20 hours of technical papers, keynote speeches and panel sessions were webcast live from Olso via a 64 kbit/s international ISDN link.
 

Fig 6. Alliance Engineering Symposium 1999 webcast live from Oslo via ISDN at 56 kbit/s

During the twelve month period ending June 1999 over 6 000 client PCs have accessed the Intranet TV site providing demonstrable evidence that IP video streaming technology works successfully.
