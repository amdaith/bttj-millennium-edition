Figure 3: Windows media technologies - true real time streaming rate 110kbit/s

Figure 3 will playback successfully on a 166Mhz multimedia PC. The effects of compression are apparent during the countdown sequence. Visual artifacts are apparent around the yellow circle, and block effects appear either side of the sweeping colour reveal. However, these impairments could be judged to be less severe than those seen in figure 2, even though Video 3 runs at a lower data rate. Again, the talking head sequence does not look too different from the original in figure 1.

To stream this video in real-time across a network would require a throughput of 110kbit/s. Internet access at this rate is possible via ISDN2 and so real-time multimedia video and audio streaming at this quality is possible for this type of content. Although there is about a 90 fold reduction of network bandwidth requirement for real-time streaming between figure 1 and Video 3, Video 3 contains most, if not all, of the original useful information contained in figure 1.
