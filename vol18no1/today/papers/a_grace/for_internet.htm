Figure 1 has not been compressed and is presented here in its original 24bit RGB digitised format. The first 8 seconds of the video are a computer generated count down sequence, the last 8 seconds are a talking head. During the countdown sequence, the sweeping motion of the blue to red colour reveal should be a smooth transition, with an audible tone synchronised to the start of each colour reveal cycle. During the talking head sequence, there should be tight lip-sync between observation of lip movement and hearing what is being said.

Figure 1: No video compression - real time streaming rate 9.835Mbit/s

It is unlikely that you will be able to play figure 1 with its original fidelity. You may observe jerky motion within the video and hear silent periods when listening to the audio track. Audio and video may also not be correctly synchronised with each other. There is a simple explaination of this: your computer does not possess the processing and graphics bandwidth needed to correctly render this clip. This illustrates another issue with multimedia content: end system performance and throughput needs to be considered by multimedia authors in addition to network performance.

To stream this video in real-time across a network would require a throughput of 9.835Mbit/s. Such a network throughput is currently an unrealistic proposition for both internet and intranet applications.
