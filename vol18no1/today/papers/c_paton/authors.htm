Process Engineering for the Alliance Partners

Authors

Colin Paton

Colin Paton joined Adastral Park at Martlesham Heath in 1989, following several years working on research in optical switching techniques. After some time working on development of test equipment for BT's TPON network, he moved into operational process development, working on technologies such as Street Multiplexors, TPON and radio systems. In 1998, he spent 9 months in Zurich, Switzerland, assisting with the development of a Business Model and operational processes for sunrise, BT's Alliance Partner in Switzerland. He is now back at Martlesham Heath, working on operational processes for new broadband switching systems, and assisting in the early stages of development of a Business Model for BTUK.

 

Alistair Goodier

Alistair Goodier joined Adastral Park at Martlesham Heath in October 1989 following graduation from Exeter University where he received a BSc (Hons) in Physics. Initially, he worked on maintaining and diagnosing faults on fibre optic networks such as TPON using advanced optical test equipment & techniques. He then moved into the field of Process Engineering where he has been involved in a wide range of operational process design, process simulation and process modelling/ publication projects. From late 1997 until the end of 1998 he worked with BT's Alliance Partners in Germany and Switzerland on the development of their Business Models. He is now back at Adastral Park working on IP Process design for IP products and network upgrades.

 

Raj Mistry

Rajendrakumar Mistry leads an operational design group in the Advanced Operational Solutions Unit, which provides solution design and consultancy to BT's Global and Joint Ventures. Following graduation in 1981, Raj joined a software house where he worked on software development for a range of telecommunications and aviation products for a number of companies including GEC (now GPT), STL, Smiths Industries and BT. In 1984 he joined a systems house to lead the development of HDLC communications protocols for remote diagnostics of computer platforms. He joined BT in 1986 where he progressed to lead a team developing an operating system, back plane protocol and device drivers for a small PBX/Switching system. In 1990, Raj undertook a part time MSc in Telecommunications and Information Systems at Essex University, which he completed successfully. He moved to the Operations and Maintenance Unit, in 1991, to work on the operational and management aspects of access technology. Since then he has led a number of areas involving technical agreement with suppliers, roll-out of fibre technology and defining operational and management requirements for a Broadband Access network, gaining agreement from the operators and suppliers involved and for down-streaming the agreed requirements into standards. He has also managed a research project looking at improving BT's ability to carry out process re-engineering efficiently and at reducing the cost of developing support systems. He is a Chartered Engineer and a member of the British Computer Society.

 

Dave Williams

Dave Williams joined BT in 1979 as an apprentice working in the North East. He went on to study a BEng in Telecommunication Engineering from the University of London. He moved to the Access Network Division at Adastral Park in 1988 working on TPON and other access technologies. He has spent several years working on network and operational process designs for BT, Concert and Alliance Partners. He is currently working on the Rubicon programme, designing the operational model, high level processes and service level agreements for the Rubicon global office(TM) product.
