Living With Agents

Authors

Jaron Collis

Jaron obtained his BSc. degree in Computation from the University of Manchester Institute of Science and technology, (UMIST), in 1993. In 1996 he completed his Ph.D. at Queen's University of Belfast on the application of artificial intelligence to quantitative scientific problem solving. He continued this work as a research fellow until he joined the Intelligent Systems Research group at BT Labs in June 1997.

Jaron is a member of the development team that produced the BCS award winning ZEUS collaborative agent building toolkit. His research interests also include the use of agents for information discovery and presentation, and agent-based electronic commerce.

 

Stuart Soltysiak

Stuart Soltysiak obtained a BSc in Command and Control, Communications and Information Systems from Cranfield Institute of Technology (1992), followed by an MSc in Cognitive Science (1993) and PhD in Computer Science from Essex University (1997).

Since joining BT in 1996, Stuart has been working on personal software agents, including machine learning of user interests and security and privacy issues related to personal agents. He is now leading an international collaborative project for agent-based information management systems. Alongside his current work with software agents, and artificial intelligence in general, Stuart retains research interests in cognition, particularly cognitive models of language processing.

 

Divine Ndumu

Divine Ndumu graduated in Civil Engineering from Imperial College in 1989, winning the Governor's prize for excellence in Civil Engineering. In 1991 he graduated in Mathematics and Computing from the Open University in 1991, and was awarded a PhD for the Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence from Imperial College in 1993. In 1992 he joined the University of Central Lancashire as a lecturer, and in 1996/7 served a one-year Research Fellowship secondment at BT at Adastral Park.

In 1997 Divine joined the Intelligent Systems Research Group at Adastral Park, and currently leads the group's software agent research programme. In 1997 the ZEUS agent-building toolkit that he co-developed won the prestigious BCS IT award. The focus of his current research is on the practical applications of AI techniques to real-world problems.

 

Nader Azarmi

Nader Azarmi is the AI technology manager within BT's Advanced Communications Engineering division and the technical manager of the Intelligent Systems Research group at Adastral Park. He joined BT in December 1989, working on the application of AI technology to telecommunications network management. He went on to set up an AI planning and scheduling R&D programme for BT's resource management. He is currently managing an international AI research and development programme covering scheduling, Agent and soft computing.

Nader has won the prestigious Institute for Operations Research and Management Sciences (INFORMS) medal-99, British Computer Society (BCS) IT medal-97, and BT Gordon Radley prize-98 for his technical work on scheduling technology and its novel applications in BT. He has also led his agent and soft computing projects to winning BCS IT award-97, BCS IT medal-98, and 5 BT technical awards.

Prior to joining BT, Nader spent many years of his academic life at Manchester, Essex, Kingston and London universities.
