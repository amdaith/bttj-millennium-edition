FreeUnet, Disruptive technology IP network research platform

Authors

Phil Clarke

Phil Clarke joined BT at Adastral Park in 1988 as a software engineer, working on network application platforms. In 1992 he moved into future applications and systems research, maintaining an interest in bridging the divide between technologies and customers. Since 1998 he has been developing and implementing advanced internet standards and protocols. He has a BSc in Software Engineering plus Psychology (Open University) and a MSc in Telecommunications Engineering (University of London), and started the world's first football website. (http://www.itfc.co.uk)

 

Jérôme Privat

Jérôme Privat joined BT at Adaastral Park in November1998 as a member of the Internet Futures team. Since then he has been one of BT representatives at the IETF (Internet Engineering Task Force) covering subjects including VoIP, integration of Circuit-switched and IP networks, and DHCP. He is a member of the FreeUnet core team. He has a degree in Physics from the National Institute of Applied Sciences (Toulouse, France) and a MSc in Physics of Laser Communications from Essex University. He is a member of Fondation George Besse.

 

Esther Sánchez Patus

Esther Sánchez Patus joined BT in 1998. She has been deeply involved in FreeUnet building and has been working in the Internet Protocol evolution (IPv6) and transition mechanisms. She is a BT IETF representative since March 1999. She has a MSc in Telecommunications Engineering by the ETSETB-UPC (Spain) and ENST Bretagne (France).

 

George Tsirtsis

George Tsirtsis joined BT in 1996, having completed an MSc at Essex University. He is working in the Internet Futures Group in ACR. His initial work was on Real Time Flow Measurements (IRTF) but then he moved on and became an expert in the areas of IPv6 and Internet Access Technologies. George is very active in the IETF with a number of Internet publications including RFC2694 and NAT-PT Internet Draft.
