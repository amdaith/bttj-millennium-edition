Adastral Park

Adastral Park, BT's Advanced Communications Technology Centre, is located in Martlesham, Suffolk - about 80 miles from Central London.

Introduction: Adastral Park derives its name from the RAF's motto - Per ardua ad astra, or through adversity to the stars. Located on land that was once part of RAF Martlesham Heath, Adastral Park now home to over 3500 of BT's employees. Click here for more information.

History: Adastral Park has a long history as a location for research and development, stretching back to 1914 when aviation research began at RAF Martlesham Heath. The Park became a centre for communications research in 1968, acquiring its present name in June 1999. Click here for more information.
