Advanced Communications Engineering (ACE)

A New Wave communications company is one which invests for the future, drives for shareholder value, is quick to move into new markets and understands the value in these markets. Its people are market focused, entrepreneurial and quick to react. A New Wave communications company cannot survive without having leading edge technology, and engineering capability, at its foundation and without clearly understanding the impact of future technology. This is the strategic vision that Advanced Communications Engineering (ACE) provides to BT. ACE leads for BT on innovation, and driving change through technology.

ACE employs some 3,500 software & network development engineers based mainly at Adastral Park, Martlesham Heath in Suffolk. The current R&D budget for ACE is some £300 million of which around 90% is earmarked for short-medium term developments focused in support of specified business objectives. ACE has evolved from the Post Office Research Establishment originally based at Dollis Hill in London. It has been the driving force behind every major technological innovation in BT's UK & Global telecommunications service and has received many prestigious awards, including no less than 5 Queen's Awards for Technological Achievement.
