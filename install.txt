To view the BT Technology Journal Millennium Edition on a Win 95, 98, NT machine:

1  Insert the CD into your CD drive. 
2  It should load up automatically. If it does not, click on Start, then Run; 
   then type in d:\setup.exe where 'd' is your CD drive.
3  The BT Technology Journal Millennium Edition requires the following browser 
   components to be viewed correctly.
   ? Internet Explorer 5
   ? Macromedia Shockwave Player v7
   ? Macromedia Flash Player v3
   ? Windows Media Player 6.4
   ? Adobe Acrobat v3 or above
   If you have all of the above items installed, go straight to step 6. If you 
   do not have all the above items, or you are not sure, go straight to step 4.
4  Click on 'Install Browser Components'. This will open a second menu.
5  Click on 'Install Internet Explorer' to install any or all of IE5, Shockwave, Flash
   and Media Player. The machine will restart.
6  After the machine reboots, the setup program should reload. If it does not, 
   repeat step 2.
7  If you need to install Adobe Acrobat, repeat step 4 and click on 'Install Adobe Acrobat'.
   Depending on your machine's current setup, you may need to restart your machine again.
   If not, click back to the first menu and then go straight to step 9.
8  After the machine reboots, the setup program should reload. If it does not, repeat step 2. 
9  Click on 'View BT Technology Journal' to view the BT Technology Journal Millennium Edition.
10 When you have finished and closed your browser, you will need to click on 'Exit' to exit 
   this program.
