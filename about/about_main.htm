About the BT Technology Journal

The BT Technology Journal is a quarterly periodical of technical papers published by Advanced Communications and Engineering (ACE) within British Telecommunications plc for the purposes of:

  • promoting an awareness, both inside and outside the business, of the breadth and excellence of the research and development undertaken by ACE,
  • fostering corporate pride in the product of ACE,
  • providing a prestigious medium for the presentation of our work to research and development workers in similar fields world-wide.

To achieve these aims, the BT Technology Journal will:

  • concentrate on theme editions which bring together a range of full length papers relating to a common subject area,
  • adopt an application-oriented and customer facing approach to the selection of themes,
  • offer rapid (2 weeks) publication of short (2-3 page) contributions of new and individual research,
  • provide opportunities also for other full length papers which do not fit into specific theme issues.

In addition, on a conceptual scale of technical standard ranging from the lowest to the highest over the world's technical literature, that of the BT Technology Journal will lie in the upper third.

The BT Technology Journal is distributed to practising engineers and senior management in companies, universities and research institutions in the UK and abroad, and widely throughout BT.
