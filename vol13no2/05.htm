Speech systems

 
K R Rose and P M Hughes


Abstract

With the new and increasingly sophisticated network services being offered by the intelligent network comes the problem of how the customers control these services. Although new telephones are being developed to assist with this problem, the majority of customers will for the foreseeable future, have only the telephone key pad/dial and handset as a means of control. As a result, the ability to provide a "user friendly" method of controlling these services via the telephone will be essential in ensuring their success and ultimately the success of the intelligent network. Using speech for communication between the customer and the network is seen as fundamental to the operation of the intelligent network. This paper reviews the role of a speech system in the telephone network, and describes how the BT speech applications platform has been designed to meet the technical systems requirements for this role. The paper concludes with an overview of how the BT speech applications platform is being developed to function as an intelligent peripheral within the intelligent network
