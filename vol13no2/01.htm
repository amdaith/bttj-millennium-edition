Intelligent networks, standards and services

 
TW Abernethy and AC Munday


Abstract

The key principle of intelligent networks is the separation of the software that controls basic switch functionality, such as setting up switch parts, from the software that controls call progression to allow advanced features to be rapidly introduced. This paper describes the current standardisation activities the IN conceptual model and IN components. It also includes a summary of some of the service available on an intelligent network, and some future developments.
