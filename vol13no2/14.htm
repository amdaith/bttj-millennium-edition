The information services supermarket - an information network prototype

 
I W Marshall and M Bagley


Abstract

The future of telecommunications is widely accepted as being convergent with that of computing. This future will include a largest number of third party service providers offering interactive multimedia services to diverse terminals over a broadband network. A prototype of the service control and management system needed to realise this capability is being built. The experiment has shown that state of the art software techniques such as open distributed processing, artificial intelligence and object orientation must be used and interface standards between networks, terminals servers and servers are also needed. This is beyond the scope of existing standards, but the designs are based on the deliverables of the TINA consortium, global telecommunications industry forum which was started in 1993, and which will be influential, it is believed, in future standards.
